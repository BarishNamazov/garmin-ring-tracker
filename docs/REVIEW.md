## What is solid

The core local-date implementation is careful: leap/month/year cases, DST gap/fold resolution, local-date cycle ordinals, strict post-28-day warning, negative countdowns, zero-day replacement, and the normal `<`/`>=` action boundaries all passed. History eviction at 24 and closed-short-interval compaction passed. The service has one final `Background.exit(null)` path, calls the installed SDK's documented `showNotification(title, subtitle, options)` signature, marks the ledger only after that call returns, and returns at most one candidate per evaluation. Manifest permissions/minimum API, scoped resources, and release annotation exclusions are correct, and the release builds are clean. Confirmations guard the foreground mutations reviewed, native menus/pickers cover button and touch conventions, and the medical strings generally avoid efficacy claims.
